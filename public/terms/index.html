Archive Restore Rehearsal helps you catalogue archive folders and record a limited restore spot check. It does not create a backup, repair files, manage RAID, or guarantee future recoverability. Keep independent copies and follow appropriate storage practices.

Read-only operation

The app requests read access to folders you choose and does not intentionally rename, edit, move, or delete source files. Its “Remove map” and “Erase local map” actions affect only records in this browser.

Identity and hash limits

Removable-drive names and saved folder handles are fallible. Confirm the physical label, folder name, expected size, and catalogue fingerprint. A successful sample proves only that the sampled files were readable and matched their recorded SHA-256 values at that time.
