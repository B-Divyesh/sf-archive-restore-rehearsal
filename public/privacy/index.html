Privacy

Archive labels, locations, file paths, hashes, saved folder permissions, and rehearsal results are stored in your browser on this device.

What leaves your device

Ordinary scanning, rehearsal, printing, export, and import use no product network request. There is no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or account system.

If you buy or verify the optional one-time unlock, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo. License verification sends the license token and product slug, not archive data.

Your control

Use “Export archive data” to download a portable JSON copy. It contains sensitive labels, paths, and hashes. “Erase local map” deletes records in this browser. Removing site data in browser settings also deletes them.

Folder permission

Supported browsers may store a read-only folder handle in IndexedDB. The browser controls this permission and may ask again. You can revoke it in browser site settings.

Contact

Questions may be sent through Sociobot. Do not include private paths or license tokens.
