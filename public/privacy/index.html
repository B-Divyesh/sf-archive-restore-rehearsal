Privacy

Archive Restore Rehearsal is local-first. The archive labels, locations, file paths, hashes, saved folder permissions, and rehearsal results you create are stored in your browser on this device. The app does not upload or analyze those records.

What leaves your device

Nothing during ordinary scanning, rehearsal, printing, export, or import. There is no product analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or account system.

If you buy or verify the optional one-time unlock, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API. The checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, and is governed by its checkout privacy notice. License verification sends only the license token and product slug. We do not send archive data with it.

Your control

Use “Export archive data” to download a portable JSON copy. It contains sensitive labels, paths, and hashes, so store it carefully. Use “Erase local map” to delete local app records. Removing site data in browser settings also deletes them. Source files on connected media are never changed by either action.

Folder permission

Supported browsers may store a read-only folder handle in IndexedDB so you can return to a drive. The browser controls this permission and may ask again. You can revoke it in browser site settings.

Contact

Questions about this product may be sent through Sociobot. Do not include private file paths or license tokens in a support message.
